Privacy policy

Daylight personal dashboard · Last updated 3 November 2026

Daylight is a personal dashboard run by its owner for their own use. It shows your calendar, mail headers, tasks, recent files and birthdays from accounts you choose to connect. This page explains what it accesses and where that information goes.

What Daylight reads

Only after you approve on Google's or Microsoft's own sign-in screen:

  • Google Calendar / Outlook Calendar: today's events (read-only).
  • Gmail: sender, subject, date and unread status of recent inbox messages; message text when you open a thread in Daylight; and sending replies you explicitly write and confirm in the app (requires the gmail.send permission). It does not delete mail or change labels unless you ask for that later.
  • Outlook Mail: sender, subject, date and unread status of recent inbox messages (read-only for now; in-app send is not available yet).
  • Google Tasks / Microsoft To Do: open tasks.
  • Google Drive: names, types and dates of recently viewed files. Never file contents.
  • Google Contacts: names and birthdays, to show upcoming birthdays.
  • Your Google email address: only when you use "Stay signed in", to check the account is allowed.

Where your data goes

  • Your calendar, mail, task, file and contact data is fetched by your browser directly from Google or Microsoft and shown on the page. It is not sent to, stored by, or shared with anyone else.
  • Personal tasks, daily notes, layout settings and your markets watchlist are saved only in your own browser.
  • Optional "Stay signed in" server: if you use it, a small server run by the dashboard owner on Cloudflare stores Google's sign-in refresh token, encrypted, together with your email address. It uses them only to give your browser short-lived access tokens. It does not read or store your calendar, mail, tasks, files or contacts. "Sign out everywhere" revokes access and deletes the stored token; unused sign-ins are deleted after 180 days.
  • Optional Email Intelligence (Gemini): off by default. Only after you explicitly consent in Connections, Daylight may send selected email text (sender, subject, body snippet) through your Daylight server to Google’s Gemini API to classify mail and extract bookings, deliveries and reminders. The Gemini API key stays on the server. Bills, payments and subscriptions are not sent unless you also enable that option. You can disable intelligence at any time. On Google’s free Gemini tier, prompts may be used to improve Google’s products — see Google’s Gemini API terms.
  • Weather: if you allow location access, your approximate coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo to get the forecast.
  • Markets: exchange rates and crypto prices are loaded from Frankfurter and CoinGecko. No personal data is sent.

Google user data

Daylight's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not used for advertising and is not sold. Optional Gemini analysis is separate from Gmail API data: when enabled, only the email excerpts you consented to process are sent to Gemini via your own Worker; Daylight does not use that data to train its own models.

Removing access

  • Choose Disconnect or Sign out everywhere in Daylight's connections panel.
  • Or remove Daylight at myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google) or myapps.microsoft.com (Microsoft).
  • Clearing this site's data in your browser removes your locally saved tasks, notes and settings.
